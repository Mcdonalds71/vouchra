[ DATA PRIVACY ]

Privacy Policy & NDPR Compliance

Vouchra adheres strictly to the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA 2023) and the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR):

  • Zero Third-Party Model Training: Uploaded invoice PDFs and waybill photos are processed in private Azure Cognitive Service instances and are never used to train public commercial AI models.
  • Encryption: Data in transit is protected using TLS 1.3 encryption; data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 with customer-managed keys (CMK) in Azure Key Vault.
