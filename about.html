The Reality of Paper Discrepancies in African Commerce

When a distributor ships 500 bags of flour or 200 cartons of cooking oil, delivery discrepancies happen every day. Drivers drop damaged units, suppliers experience depot stockouts, and store clerks record handwritten variances on physical paper waybills.

The failure occurs weeks later: the corporate accounting office receives an electronic or printed invoice from the supplier listing the original 500 bags. Without an automated system tying the receiving clerk’s physical gate stamp to the invoice line items, the enterprise pays for goods it never received.

We engineered Vouchra to eliminate this vulnerability entirely. By running optical layout intelligence over mobile gate photos and matching them in real time against purchase contracts, we protect enterprise balance sheets before funds leave the bank.
