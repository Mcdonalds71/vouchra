Frequently Asked Questions

How does Vouchra handle carbon-copy or folded paper waybills?

Our Azure AI Document Intelligence model is trained specifically on handwritten gate stamps, folded receipt carbon copies, and smartphone photos captured under warehouse dock lighting. It extracts line items with 99.4% field accuracy.

Do gate receiving clerks require complex software training?

No. Gate clerks don't even need computers. They simply snap a photograph of the stamped goods received note using our WhatsApp Ingest Bot or mobile Progressive Web App. The AI matches it against the vendor's PO and invoice automatically.

Does Vouchra integrate with our existing accounting ERP?

Yes. Vouchra provides bi-directional connectors for SAP Business One, Oracle NetSuite, Sage Evolution, QuickBooks Online, and Microsoft Dynamics 365, alongside comprehensive REST APIs and daily CSV reconciliation exports.

Can Nigerian corporate clients settle subscriptions in Naira (₦)?

Yes. While plans are denominated in USD for regional Pan-African consistency, Nigerian clients can settle monthly or annually in Naira at prevailing official NAFEM rates via commercial bank transfer.
